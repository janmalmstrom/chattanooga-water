## 13. Local Intelligence

### Neighborhoods & Areas
- **North Chattanooga** (1920s-1950s homes): older galvanized/copper pipes, basement flooding common from heavy rains
- **Hixson** (northwest suburbs, newer): sump pump failures common, finished basements = high damage cost
- **Red Bank** (adjacent to Hixson): mix of older and newer homes, storm runoff issues
- **Ooltewah** (eastern Hamilton County, fast-growing): newer construction, drainage issues in new subdivisions
- **East Brainerd** (southeast, suburban): fastest growing, appliance failures in newer homes
- **Signal Mountain** (elevated): winter pipe freeze risk at higher elevation, mountain runoff flooding
- **Lookout Valley** (near Lookout Mountain): Tennessee River proximity, flooding risk from river and Lookout Creek
- **St. Elmo** (historic, base of Lookout Mountain): older homes, cast iron drain lines
- **Missionary Ridge**: ridge-top runoff, older homes on slopes prone to seepage
- **Soddy-Daisy** (north, Chickamauga Lake shore): TVA lake flooding, older housing stock

### Regional Specifics
- **Tennessee River & TVA:** The Chickamauga Dam (TVA) is upstream of Chattanooga. TVA manages water levels — during heavy rain events, controlled releases can raise river levels. Downtown waterfront and Lookout Valley are most affected.
- **Chattanooga Creek:** Flows through South Chattanooga into the Tennessee River. Known flooding history, especially in spring and after heavy rain events.
- **Humid subtropical climate:** Average 54 inches of rain/year (vs 37 in Chicago). High year-round humidity means mold establishes faster — 24-hour response is more critical than in drier climates.
- **Storm season:** March–May for severe thunderstorms and tornadoes. August–October for Gulf Coast tropical system remnants tracking up I-65/I-24 corridor. Both bring significant water damage events.
- **Signal Mountain freeze risk:** At 2,100+ ft elevation, Signal Mountain gets measurable winter ice and snow that downtown Chattanooga does not. Pipes freeze on Signal Mountain when downtown temps are above freezing.
- **Tennessee HIC license:** Tennessee requires a Home Improvement Contractor (HIC) license for residential work over $3,000. Always verify before hiring.
- **Volkswagen Chattanooga:** VW assembly plant is the city's largest employer. Engineering and management staff = high-income homeowners with good insurance and professional documentation expectations.
- **CHI Memorial & Erlanger Health:** Two major hospital systems. Medical office buildings = commercial water damage clients.
- **Older housing stock:** North Chattanooga, St. Elmo, and Missionary Ridge have significant pre-1960 housing with older water supply lines.
