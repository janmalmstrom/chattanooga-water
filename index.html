What We Do

Water Damage Restoration Services — Chattanooga, TN

Storm flooding at midnight. Basement flood from Tennessee River backup. Sewage through the floor drain. Burst pipe on Signal Mountain in January. Every situation is different — here is what the contractors we connect you with handle.

Water Extraction

Truck-mounted extraction units remove standing water trapped in carpet, pad, and hard floors. Contractors follow with thermal imaging to find hidden moisture in wall cavities. In Chattanooga's humid subtropical climate, fast extraction is critical — high ambient humidity accelerates mold conditions.

Structural Drying

Industrial dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers placed per IICRC S500 psychrometric calculations. Daily moisture readings documented. Chattanooga's high ambient humidity makes equipment sizing more critical than in drier states — undersized dehumidifiers leave structures vulnerable to mold.

Storm Flooding Cleanup

Chattanooga averages 54 inches of rain per year. Severe thunderstorms and Gulf Coast tropical remnants tracking up I-24 frequently cause interior flooding. Ground water entering a structure is Category 3 — contractors follow IICRC S500 protocol for contaminated water cleanup.

Tennessee River Flooding

TVA's Chickamauga Dam controls water levels on the Tennessee River. During heavy rain, controlled releases raise river levels affecting Lookout Valley and Soddy-Daisy along Chickamauga Lake. Tennessee River overbank flooding typically requires NFIP flood coverage — not standard homeowners insurance.

Sewage & Contaminated Water

Category 3 black water cannot be dried in place — contaminated materials must be removed per IICRC S500. Contractors handle containment, removal, EPA-registered antimicrobial treatment, and HEPA air scrubbing. In Chattanooga's humid climate, any delay with Category 3 dramatically increases mold risk.

Mold Remediation

Chattanooga's year-round humidity means mold can establish faster after water damage than in northern states. Contractors assess, contain, and remediate per IICRC S520. Independent clearance testing available through a certified industrial hygienist.

Commercial Water Damage

Office buildings, medical facilities, and commercial properties in the Chattanooga metro. Contractors staff commercial jobs to minimize downtime and provide the documentation Hamilton County insurance adjusters require.

Insurance Claim Assistance

The contractors we connect you with work with State Farm, Tennessee Farm Bureau, USAA, Erie Insurance, Nationwide, Allstate. Complete moisture logs, photos, and Xactimate-compatible scope. Tennessee Farm Bureau adjusters know Chattanooga restoration pricing. Contractors communicate directly with your adjuster.

Why Speed Matters

Water Damage Gets Worse Every Hour — Especially in Chattanooga's Humidity

Chattanooga's humid subtropical climate compresses the damage timeline. The 24–48 hour mold window in IICRC S520 assumes average indoor conditions — Chattanooga's high ambient humidity makes that window shorter in summer.

1h

First Hour: Spread & Absorption

Water migrates under flooring, into wall cavities, and through drywall. The longer it sits in Chattanooga's heat, the more surface area is affected and the more material must be replaced.

24h

24 Hours: Drywall Damage and Mold Risk

Drywall swells and loses integrity. In Chattanooga's heat and humidity, the 24-hour mark is when mold risk becomes acute. Older homes in North Chattanooga and St. Elmo are especially vulnerable. A $3,000 job can become $10,000+.

48h

48 Hours: Mold Growth Likely

IICRC S520 identifies 24–48 hours as the mold growth window. In Chattanooga's subtropical humidity, this window can be shorter in summer months. Once mold is present, remediation scope and cost increase significantly.

7d

One Week: Structural Compromise

Framing lumber and subfloor OSB begin to deteriorate. Older homes in North Chattanooga and Missionary Ridge are especially vulnerable to prolonged saturation.
