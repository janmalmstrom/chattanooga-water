First Response

Water Extraction

Extraction is the first and most critical step. In Chattanooga's humid subtropical climate, fast extraction is especially important — ambient humidity slows evaporation and accelerates mold conditions. Contractors use truck-mounted extraction units capable of removing thousands of gallons per hour.

For Chattanooga's older homes in North Chattanooga and St. Elmo with finished basements, water hides under hardwood flooring, in subfloor OSB, and in wall cavities. After extraction, thermal imaging cameras locate hidden moisture before drying equipment is placed.

What We Use

  • Truck-mounted extraction units
  • Portable extractors for difficult access
  • Hardwood floor mat extraction systems
  • Thermal imaging cameras
  • Professional moisture meters

What Gets Extracted

  • Standing water
  • Water in carpet and pad
  • Surface water on hard floors
  • Water in subfloor materials
  • Water in wall cavities

What Happens Next

  • Thermal imaging sweep for hidden moisture
  • Decision: dry vs remove
  • Drying equipment setup
  • Moisture readings documented for insurance
IICRC S500 Standard

Structural Drying

Chattanooga's high ambient humidity (averaging 67%) makes equipment sizing more critical than in drier states. Undersized dehumidifiers fail to maintain the psychrometric conditions required for proper drying — the result is incomplete drying and mold growth. Daily moisture readings are taken at documented points and a full drying report is provided at completion.

Equipment Used

  • LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers
  • High-velocity air movers
  • Injectidry wall cavity drying systems
  • Floor drying mats
  • Desiccant dehumidifiers (severe cases)

Monitoring & Documentation

  • Daily moisture readings at mapped points
  • Psychrometric data logged daily
  • Progress photos for insurance
  • Drying complete when materials reach target MC
  • Drying report provided at completion

Typical Timeline

  • Category 1 (clean water): 3–5 days
  • Category 2 (gray water): 4–7 days
  • Category 3 (black water): requires demolition first
  • Hardwood floors: 7–14 days (if salvageable)
  • High humidity may extend timelines
Chattanooga's Primary Water Risk

Storm Flooding & Heavy Rain Water Damage

Chattanooga averages 54 inches of rain per year. Severe thunderstorms in spring, flash floods from Mountain Runoff (Lookout Mountain and Signal Mountain channel storm water rapidly into lower residential areas), and Gulf Remnants — Gulf Coast tropical systems tracking up I-24 in late summer — are the primary causes of water damage in Chattanooga homes.

Storm water entering a structure from outside is classified as Category 3 (black water) under IICRC S500 — it cannot be dried in place. Standard Tennessee homeowners insurance covers sudden storm damage above grade, but water entering through foundation or ground flooding typically requires NFIP flood coverage.

Common Storm Damage Sources

  • Window and door seals overwhelmed by driving rain
  • Roof leaks from wind-driven storm damage
  • Mountain runoff overwhelming yard drainage
  • Sump pump failure during heavy rain
  • Flash flooding near Chattanooga Creek

What We Do

  • Rapid water extraction
  • Category 3 assessment and protocol
  • Contaminated material removal
  • Structural drying of remaining materials
  • Moisture log for insurance claim

While Waiting for Us

  • Move valuables above water level
  • Turn off electricity in affected areas
  • Do not use a regular vacuum for standing water
  • Call your insurance carrier to open a claim
  • Document damage with photos before cleanup
Category 3 — Black Water Protocol

Sewage Cleanup & Contaminated Water Remediation

Sewage backup, toilet overflow, and floodwater from outside are classified as Category 3 (black water) under IICRC S500. In Chattanooga's heat and humidity, Category 3 situations require immediate action to prevent rapid mold establishment. Standard Tennessee homeowners policies often exclude sewer backup unless you have a sewer/water backup rider.

Category 3 Sources

  • Sewage backup through floor drains or toilets
  • Floodwater from outside the structure
  • Groundwater intrusion through foundation
  • Category 1/2 water left untreated 72+ hours

Materials That Must Be Removed

  • Drywall that contacted Category 3 water
  • Carpet and pad
  • Insulation
  • Flooring with porous backing
  • Any organic material that cannot be disinfected

Our Protocol

  • Containment and PPE
  • Removal of Category 3 materials
  • EPA-registered disinfectant application
  • HEPA air scrubbing
  • Structural drying of remaining materials
  • Documentation for insurance or self-pay
IICRC S520 Standard

Mold Remediation

Mold can begin growing on wet organic materials within 24–48 hours. In Chattanooga's humid subtropical climate with high summer temperatures and humidity, mold can establish significantly faster than in drier climates. Water damage dried with consumer fans rather than professional equipment frequently develops mold within days in a Chattanooga summer.

Signs You May Have Mold

  • Musty odor in a specific area
  • Past water damage not professionally dried
  • Visible discoloration on drywall or wood
  • Water stain "dried out" with fans
  • Persistent musty smell after flooding

Remediation Protocol

  • Assessment and scope development
  • Containment (negative air pressure)
  • Removal of mold-contaminated materials
  • HEPA vacuuming of structural surfaces
  • Antimicrobial treatment
  • Air scrubbing with HEPA filtration

Insurance & Mold in Tennessee

  • Mold from a covered water event may be covered
  • Pre-existing mold typically excluded
  • Mold from gradual leaks often excluded
  • The contractor documents the mold's origin for your adjuster
  • Clearance testing available on request
Claims Navigation

Insurance Claim Assistance

Most Tennessee homeowners insurance covers sudden and accidental water damage. Ground flooding from outside typically requires NFIP or private flood policy. Sewer backup requires a specific endorsement. The contractors we connect you with work with State Farm, Tennessee Farm Bureau, USAA, Erie Insurance, Nationwide, and all major Tennessee carriers. Tennessee Farm Bureau adjusters are familiar with Chattanooga restoration market pricing.

What We Provide

  • Daily moisture reading logs
  • Before, during, and after photos
  • Xactimate-compatible scope of work
  • Equipment inventory and placement logs
  • Completed drying report
  • Direct communication with your adjuster

Typically Covered (TN Policies)

  • Burst or frozen pipe
  • Appliance failure (sudden)
  • Sudden roof leak from storm
  • Accidental overflow (tub, sink)
  • Storm wind-driven rain through opening

Often Excluded

  • Gradual leaks / maintenance issues
  • Ground flooding (without flood policy)
  • Sewer backup (without endorsement)
  • Mold from pre-existing conditions
  • Tennessee River overbank flooding (NFIP)
